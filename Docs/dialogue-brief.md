# The Witch — Dialogue Brief

**The Witch:** an ancient, rootbound protector of a forest that has been broken and is angry. Terse, archaic, protective; her familiars (Owl, Cat, Bear, Crow, Rat, Wolf) are kin, not tools. Existing lines: "Tarry not." / "There is work to be done." / "The woods are broken. They are angry."

## Who's speaking

**Darv, "The Hoarder."** Folksy junk-collector who trades relics from his pile and drops his g's ("somethin'", "ya"). Warm, chatty, always holding on to something from your past.

**Neow, "Mother of Resurrection."** The one who brought you back and sent you up to kill the Architect; speaks in breathy two-word fragments ("..arise.. my.. warrior..."). Maternal on the surface, cold underneath.

**Nonupeipe, "Serendipity Incarnate."** A vain, gracious sovereign who dresses visitors in finery and calls them her champion. Formal sentences, no slang, sharp edge under the courtesy.

**Orobas, "Living Rainbow."** A childlike creature speaking in two-word bursts ("Shiny!! Violence bad!") who upgrades your starting gear. Pacifist, endlessly curious, easily frightened.

**Pael, "Melting Dragon."** A drowsy dragon who gives away pieces of his own flesh and calls the Architect "Father." Every line trails off toward sleep; he wants peace, not vengeance.

**Tanx, "Khimera King."** ALL CAPS, all the time; hands out weapons and demands "FIGHT ME, WITCH!!" Judges everyone by what they hit things with.

**Tezcatara, "It Which Feeds the Fire."** A doting grandmother ("sweetie", "dear") who offers soup and burns away your flaws. One sinister word per line is stressed; she knows your secret.

**Vakuu, "The First Demon."** A smooth contract-maker offering power at a price, courteous and bored. Reminds you that you read the fine print.

**The Architect.** The tired ruler at the top of the Spire, curt and contemptuous of the intrusion. Short sentences only; snaps to rage when pushed.

## Themes

- The Spire has reopened and is burning; Neow's resurrected "puppets" climb it to kill the Architect.
- Every Ancient has a stance on that plan: gleeful (Tezcatara), transactional (Vakuu), opposed (Orobas, Pael), indifferent (Darv, Tanx).
- Each Ancient knows the visitor's past and hints at it; the Witch needs a secret of her own (her woods, her companions, a bargain).
- Old things recognizing old things: the Witch is older than most of them.
- Open question to settle first: why is the Witch in the Spire, and what broke the woods?
