Method and caveats
  • Source: Docs/card-data/*.json (Witch parsed from the card classes; base classes from the decompiled game). Rebuild with node Docs/card-data/balance.js.
  • Effective damage = per-hit damage × parsed hit count ("twice" / "N times" in the damage sentence) × the AoE multiplier when the card hits ALL enemies.
  • "Flat" cards have a fixed printed number. Scaling ("for each", "equal to"), X-cost, conditional-hit, and per-turn engine cards are counted but never averaged.
  • Witch Token / Status / Special cards and multiplayer-only cards are excluded unless "Include Witch tokens" is on. Base classes have no token layer.
  • Regent star-cost cards stay in the numbers here; they are subsidized by a second currency and read as above-rate.
  • Riders, potions, familiars, and synergy are invisible to this page. It surfaces shape and outliers, not balance.
