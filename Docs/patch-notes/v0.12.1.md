# The Witch v0.12.1

## Reworks
- **Mulch** – Now adds one random card per card actually Exhausted (was always X cards, even if you Exhausted fewer).
- **Incant** – Upgrade now +3 Block (was +1 Hex).
- **Smolder** – Upgrade now +1 turn of Ember Jars (was +2 Block).
- **Wax and Wane** – Upgrade now +1 Hex (was +3 Block).
- **Cloak of Moonlight** – Upgrade now costs 1 (was Innate).
- **Polymorph** – Now Exhausts.

## Changes
- **Moth Familiar** – Cost 2 → 1.
- **Star Dust** – Moonlight 4 → 3 (upgrade +2 → +1).
- **Gossamer** – Block 4 → 3.
- **Animal Handling** – Familiar cards 3 → 2.

## Other
- Russian translation added – thank you Klomer!
- Forbidden Magic now glows gold while an enemy is Hexed.
- Unstable potion tooltip now mentions right-click to use.
