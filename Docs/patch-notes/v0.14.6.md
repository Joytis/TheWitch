# The Witch v0.14.6
## Changes
- **Mulch** – Now has Exhaust.
- **Rotting Roots** – Each Rotting Roots played adds 1 to the Hex gained per turn (was always 1).

## Other
- **Message in a Bottle** – The bottled card now keeps the Replay, keywords and increased numbers it gained in combat.
- **Mushroom Extract** – Fixed disguised cards sometimes showing their real art. A card whose draw is prevented no longer stays disguised.
