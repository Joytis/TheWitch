# The Witch v0.12.0

## New
- **Moth Familiar** – Common 2-cost Power. Summons a Moth that each turn adds either **Star Dust** (apply 4 Moonlight, 6 when upgraded) or **Gossamer** (gain 4 Block, 5 when upgraded) to your hand.
- **Cloak of Twilight** – 1-cost Uncommon Power. Whenever you trigger Hex, gain 2 Block (3 when upgraded). This is the old Cloak of Moonlight effect.
- **Moonlight** – New debuff. At the start of your turn, the target takes damage equal to its Moonlight. Stacks never decay.

## Reworks
- **Cloak of Moonlight** – Uncommon 2-Cost Power: Whenever you create a card or Potion, apply 1 Moonlight to a random enemy. Upgrade grants Innate
- **Moonbeam** – Deal 10 damage, apply 10 Moonlight (12 / 12 when upgraded).
- **Salt and Ash** – Was a 2-cost Attack (12 damage to ALL, stabilize a Potion, Exhaust). Now a 1-cost Skill: gain 6 Block, whenever you play a Skill this turn deal 3 damage to ALL enemies (8 Block / 4 damage when upgraded).
- **Smolder** – Now creates an Unstable Ember Jar at the start of each of the next 2 turns instead of one immediately. Block 5 → 4 (upgrade +3 → +2).
- **Wild Growth** – No longer doubles Brambles. Now: at the start of each of the next 2 turns, gain 1 Energy and 5 Brambles. Cost 1 → 2, no longer Exhausts (upgrade adds a turn instead of reducing cost).
- **Mulch** – Now always adds X cards even if fewer were exhausted.
- **Hasty Brew** – Upgrade now costs 0 instead of removing Exhaust.
- **Ferocity** – Upgrade now costs 0 instead of +2 damage.
- **Pocket Rats** – Upgrade now adds Rats+ instead of an extra Rats.
- **Strike Fear** – Upgrade now only adds +1 Vulnerable (no longer +2 damage).
- **Wax and Wane** – Upgrade now +3 Block (was +1 Block and +1 Hex).
- **Share the Brew** – Potions granted are now Unstable.
- **Wicker Consumation** – Can now be upgraded (+5 damage). Wicker Bones+ creates Wicker Consumation+.
- **Fertilizer** / **Prickly Vial** – Brambles now go to the targeted player in multiplayer.
- **Flock** – Renamed to **Animal Handling**.
- **Big Batch**, **Pact of Agony** – Removed.

## Changes
- **Bottle Barrage** – Cost 1 → 2.
- **Call the Pack** – Upgrade Block +2 → +3.
- **Creeping Vines** – Uncommon → Rare. Brambles 5 → 6.
- **Deep Roots** – Brambles per turn 3 → 2.
- **Needle Whip** – Damage 9 → 8.
- **Pack Tactics** – Strength 2 → 3.
- **Polymorph** – Rare → Uncommon.
- **Sprouts** – Upgrade Brambles +2 → +1.
- **Volatile Vapors** – Damage 4 → 3 (upgrade +2 → +1).

## New Art
- **Weathered Witch Hat** – Final card art.
- **Moth Familiar**, **Star Dust**, **Gossamer**, **Cloak of Twilight** – New card art (plus the Moth pet sprite).
- **Animal Handling**, **Vile Renewal**, **Blood Anointment**, **Plague Tide** – New card art.

## Other

- Fixed Buddy in a Bottle rolling multiplayer-only summons (Plague Tide) in singleplayer.
- Fixed a hang on save-and-quit / return to menu after any combat with a Message in a Bottle card bottled.
- Wording pass across cards, powers, and relics for consistency with the base game.
