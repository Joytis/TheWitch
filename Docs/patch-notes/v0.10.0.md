# The Witch v0.10.0

## New
- **Wicker Heart** – New Rare Skill (1 Energy, Retain, Exhaust): Gain 5 Brambles. If you have 10 Brambles, add Wicker Bones to your Hand (upgrade: 7 Brambles, adds Wicker Bones+).
- **Wicker Bones** – Created by Wicker Heart (1 Energy, Exhaust): Gain 12 Block. If you have 30 Brambles, add Wicker Consumation to your Hand (upgrade: 16 Block).
- **Wicker Consumation** – Created by Wicker Bones (1 Energy, Exhaust): Deal 20 damage. If you have at least 50 Brambles, gain Incarnation.
- **Incarnation** – New Power: Whenever you play a card, gain 5 Brambles.
- **Vile Renewal** – New Uncommon Skill (1 Energy): Gain 5 Block. Attacks do not remove Hex or Brambles this turn (upgrade: 8 Block).
- **Flock** – New Uncommon Skill (1 Energy): Create 3 random Familiar cards (upgrade: 4).
- **Unstable Potions tip** – A one-time tutorial pop-up now explains Unstable potions and right-click quick use the first time you create one.

## Reworks
- **Tasty Herbs** – Now: Whenever you use an Unstable potion, 25% chance it is used an additional time (was: heal 3 HP at the end of combat if you used a potion).
- **Eye of Newt** – Creates an Unstable Noxious Brew instead of an Unstable Ember Jar.
- **Cloak of Moonlight** – Block gained from Hex triggers is no longer modified by Dexterity.

## Changes
- **Ambush** – Cost 2 → 1.
- **Extract Life** – Cost 2 → 1.
- **Eye of Newt** – Cost 2 → 1.
- **Brambleburst** – Cost 3 → 2, Rare → Uncommon, damage per Bramble 2 → 1.
- **Blood Anointment** – Uncommon → Rare.
- **Puff of Smoke** – Block 4 → 3.

## New Art
- **Bind in Blood**, **Forbidden Magic**, **Read the Bones** Final card art.

## Other
- **Removed:** Impale, Broken Pact, Bottle Bombardment.
- Fixed Mushroom Extract sometimes showing a card's real title and text.
