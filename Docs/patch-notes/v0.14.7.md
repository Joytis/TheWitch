# The Witch v0.14.7
## Changes
- **Plague Tide** – Cost 2 → 3.
- **Smolder** – Upgrade now gives Block 4 → 6 instead of Ember Jar turns 2 → 3.
- **Buddies in a Bottle** (was Buddy in a Bottle) – Adds 2 different random Familiar summon cards to your hand instead of 1.
- **Separatory Funnel** – Now a Rare relic. Whenever an effect would create a random Potion, choose 1 of 3 random Potions instead (was: choose any Potion from the pool).
- **Sack of Treats** – Now an Ancient relic: it replaces Separatory Funnel as the Touch of Orobas upgrade of Large Pockets. No longer a random drop.
- **Tasty Herbs** – Reworked: every 4th Potion you use is used an additional time (was: 25% chance for Unstable Potions). Shows a counter.
- **Wormy's Apple** – Max HP 10 → 13. Now shows Wormy in its tooltip.

## Other
- **Separatory Funnel** – Now also applies to Share the Brew, Alchemize and Entropic Brew.
- Potions used again by Tasty Herbs or replayed from a bottle now count as a Potion used this turn.
- Fixed the Witch sometimes freezing on an animation instead of returning to idle at low HP.
- Right-click potion quick use works alongside the new Quick Potions mod.
