# The Witch v0.12.2

## New
- **Colorful Philosophers** – The statues can now argue for the Witch: other characters may be offered "Obtain 3 Witch cards" at the event.

## Changes
- **Ritual Casting** – Now stacks. Each copy frees one more random card in hand whenever you play a card costing 2 or more.
- **Bind in Blood** – Now costs 1 HP to play.
- **Cloak of Moonlight** – Cost 2 → 1. Upgrade now grants Innate (was cost reduction).
- **Wisdom** (Owl) – Now Draw 2 Discard 1. Upgrade now draws 3 and discards 2 (was draw 2, discard 1).
- **Swarm** (Rat) –  Now adds 2 Rats to your Draw Pile and Discard Pile No longer adds a Rats to your hand. (upgrade 3).
- **Wicker Bones** – Gains Retain.
- **Wicker Consumation** – Gains Retain.

## Changes
- **Tinder** – Cost 1 → 0.
- **Creeping Vines** – Brambles 6 → 5 (upgrade +2 → +1).
- **Stuck in a Bush** – Brambles 7 → 8.
- **Wild Growth** – Brambles 5 → 6.
- **Extract Life** – Damage 12 → 10.
- **Needle Whip** – Damage 8 → 9.
- **Star Dust** – Moonlight 3 → 4.
- **Volatile Vapors** – Stacks 3 → 4.

## Other
- Fixed Tasty Herbs freezing combat when replaying a potion that had already left the belt.
- Token potions (Noxious Brew, Ember Jar, Ominous Flask, etc.) no longer drop from events that pick from the full potion pool.
