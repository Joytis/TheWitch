# The Witch v0.13.0

## New
- **Familiar keyword** – Familiar token cards now carry a **Familiar** keyword, so "Familiar card" payoffs (Overrun, Animal Handling, Command, …) are readable on the card face. Summon cards, payoffs, and familiar relics show a shared tooltip explaining how familiars work.
- **Artist credits** – Card tooltips now show who drew each card's art ("Art by …"). Can be turned off with the new **Artist Credits** mod setting.

## Reworks
- **A Little Sip** – No longer grants Strength; now just draws a card whenever you use a Potion. Now Ethereal; upgrade removes Ethereal (was +1 Strength).
- **Bonfire** – Can now exhaust Ash.
- **Tinder** – Can now exhaust Ash.
- **Witchcraft** – Upgrade now grants Retain (was +1 Potion).

## New Art
- **Call the Pack**, **Herbal Brew**, **Primal Form**, **Toil and Trouble** – Final card art.

## Other
- **Overrun** now shows as a debuff on the trampled enemy, displaying how much damage each Familiar card will deal to it.
- **Bottled Message** now keeps a card's grown stats (e.g. Capture Soul's bonus Hex) when bottled and unbottled.
- Familiar buff tooltips now name the two cards each familiar alternates between.
- **Buddy in a Bottle** description clarified: it adds a card that summons a Familiar.
