# The Witch v0.14.0
## Changes
- **A Little Sip** – Whenever you use a Potion, now also gain 1 Brambles (still draws a card).
- **Animal Handling** – +1 Card Created. Upgrade now makes the added Familiar cards Upgraded (was +1 card).
- **Bottomless Cauldron** – Upgrade now creates an Unstable Noxious Brew when played (was cost 2 → 1).
- **Moonbeam** – Applies Moonlight equal to the damage dealt (was a flat 10 (12)).
- **Mulch** – The cards it adds are now Upgraded.
- **Smolder** / **Wild Growth** – Playing another copy now starts its own countdown instead of extending the existing one: two copies give double the payoff over the shorter span.
- **Swarm** – Now also increases Rats damage by 1.
- **Wisdom** – No longer makes you discard. Draw 2 (3).
- **Bottle Wall** – Block 8 → 6 (12 → 10).
- **Embrace the Wilds** – Draw 1 fewer card each turn (was 2).
- **Extract Life** – Upgrade now gives +5 Damage (was +1 Replay).
- **Gossamer** – Block 3 → 5 (4 → 6).
- **Hide in a Bush** – Block 6 → 7 (8 → 9).
- **Needle Whip** – Damage 9 → 10 (11 → 12).
- **Noxious Brew** – Damage 8 → 7.
- **Refuse Pile** – Adds 2 Rats to each pile (was 1).
- **Sprouts** – Block 3 → 4 (5 → 6).
- **Volatile Vapors** – Damage 4 → 3 (5 → 4).
- **Wicker Consumation** – Damage 20 → 24 (25 → 29).
- **Wicker Heart** – Brambles 5 → 6 (7 → 8).

## New Art
- **Animated Witch** – The Witch is now a Live2D model in combat, at rest sites, and in the shop.
- **Gnash**, **Incant**, **Owl Familiar**, **Sprouts**, **Vile Renewal**, **Witchcraft** – Final card art.

## Other
- **Full translations** – The Witch is now fully translated into Simplified Chinese, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, and Spanish. Corrections are welcome on ParaTranz.
- **Moth Familiar** – Shows up in the shop's Power slot much less often (~10% of shops, was over half). Card reward odds are unchanged.
- Multiplayer: a non-Witch player who applies Hex (e.g. with Witch cards gained another way) can now trigger it with their own Attacks.
- The potion choice screen now widens and scrolls when other mods add many potions to the pool.
