# The Witch v0.13.1

## New Art
- **Crystal Bottle**, **Cursed Spellbook**, **Eye of Newt**, **Tinder** – Final card art.

## Other
- Familiar cards now have their summon card's rarity colors (banner, border, and title).
- Improved compatibility with other mods: familiar pets no longer collide with other mods' creatures of the same name, the Witch's card trail works alongside RitsuLib mods, and one failing patch (e.g. from a conflicting mod) no longer disables the rest of the Witch.
- Artist credits updated.
