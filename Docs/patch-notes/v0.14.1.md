# The Witch v0.14.1

## Reworks
- **Ambush!** – Now deals 5 (8) damage to ALL enemies, +1 for each of your Brambles (was a flat 9 (12)). Still plays itself for free when drawn.

## Changes
- **Animal Handling** – Random Familiar cards are now weighted: cards from Rare familiars show up less often, and Knowledge and Rummage are the rarest rolls (was an even pick).

## New Art
- **Knowledge**, **Wisdom** – Final card art.
