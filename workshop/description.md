# The Witch

*The woods are broken. They are angry.*

In active development. Expect regular balance changes and some placeholder card art.

**Languages:** English. Community translations (Chinese, German, French, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Spanish) are in progress on ParaTranz - see *Help translate* below.

# Starting Kit

- 4× **Strike**
- 4× **Defend**
- 1× **Harvest** - deal damage and brew a small Unstable potion.
- 1× **Incant** - gain Block; if you drank a potion this turn, apply Hex.
- Starting relic: **Large Pockets** - extra potion slots. You will need them.

# Potions

The Witch makes her own potions mid-fight. Cards brew all sorts of potions, most of them **Unstable** - gone at the end of combat. A fun way to stack effects across turns! (I've also added a little patch which plays potions immediatley on Right-Click!)

!!NEED IMAGE HERE!!

# Hex

A curse that makes every Attack hurt more - **+2 damage per Hex** - and fades one stack each time the target is Hit! Stack it up, then cash in with an attack, or just use it as a nifty way to chip in with damage :) 

!!NEED IMAGE HERE!!

# Familiars

Summon little buddies during combat! At the start of every turn, each familiar gives you a card! Each familiar has their own two unique cards. All sorts of decks want familiars, so don't be shy bringing one along <33

!!NEED IMAGE HERE!!

# Brambles

Grow some BIG OLE THICKETS and hunker down! Every attack that hits you deals damage back, then removes one bramble (like thorns, but strictly worse). Build up some block, grow a big ole wall of spikes, and let the spire do the work for you :flex_emoji:x100
