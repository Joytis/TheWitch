[h1]Potions[/h1]

The Witch makes her own potions mid-fight. Cards brew all sorts of potions, most of them [b]Unstable[/b] - gone at the end of combat. A fun way to stack effects across turns! (I've also added a little patch which plays potions immediatley on Right-Click!)

[h1]Hex[/h1]

A curse that makes every Attack hurt more - [b]+2 damage per Hex[/b] - and fades one stack each time the target is Hit! Stack it up, then cash in with an attack, or just use it as a nifty way to chip in with damage :) 

[h1]Familiars[/h1]

Summon little buddies during combat! At the start of every turn, each familiar gives you a card! Each familiar has their own two unique cards. All sorts of decks want familiars, so don't be shy bringing one along <33

[h1]Brambles[/h1]

Grow some BIG OLE THICKETS and hunker down! Every attack that hits you deals damage back, then removes one bramble (like thorns, but strictly worse). Build up some block, grow a big ole wall of spikes, and let the spire do the work for you :flex_emoji:x100

[h1]Custom Art[/h1]

Every finished card, relic, potion, and familiar is hand-drawn (Art is hard and we're doing out best <3). Cards still on placeholders are being replaced piece by piece - the current state of the art pass is public at the [url=https://joytis.github.io/TheWicken/pages/art-tracker.html]art tracker[/url].

[b]No AI-generated art is used anywhere in The Witch.[/b]
