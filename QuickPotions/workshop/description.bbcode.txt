[h1]Quick Potions[/h1]
[b]Right-click a potion on your belt to use it instantly.[/b]

No Use/Discard popup, no targeting arrow:
[list]
[*][b]Enemy-targeted potions[/b] are thrown at a random living enemy.
[*][b]Self-targeted and untargeted potions[/b] are used immediately.
[*]The rare potions that need a pick (e.g. one aimed at an ally) skip the popup and go straight to targeting.
[/list]

Left-click works exactly as before, so Discard is still one click away.

Works with every character, including modded ones. Doesn't affect gameplay: you can join multiplayer lobbies whose other players don't have it.

[hr][/hr]
Originally a quality-of-life feature of [b]The Witch[/b] character mod, split out by request. The Witch still has it built in; installing both is fine.
